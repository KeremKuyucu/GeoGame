# Product Context: GeoGame

## 1. Why GeoGame Exists
Geography trivia applications frequently suffer from repetitive formats (simple flag flashcards), clunky interfaces, or heavy reliance on always-online servers. GeoGame solves this by providing:
- Diverse game modes spanning spatial geometry, political adjacency, heraldry, and cartography.
- An interactive, polished Flutter experience with vector-based map rendering and fluid animations.
- Offline play capability that allows users to test their geographical knowledge without an active connection.

## 2. User Experience Goals
- **Intuitive Gameplay**: Visual and haptic feedback on answers, clear directional compass pointers in distance mode, and interactive zoom/pan capabilities on maps.
- **Fair & Adaptive Difficulty**: Filter game modes by continent or difficulty level, with options for button-based multiple choice or text-based direct input.
- **Progression & Achievement**: Global leaderboards, XP accumulation, daily streaks, and country collection tracking that rewards exploration and continuous learning.
- **Inclusive Multilingual Support**: Fully localized in English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, and Russian.

## 3. Supported Game Modes
1. **Capital City**: Identify world capitals from country prompts.
2. **Flag Identification**: Recognize national flags.
3. **Distance & Bearing**: Guess countries using kilometer distance and compass direction vectors.
4. **Borderline**: Determine whether two nations share a territorial land border.
5. **BorderPath**: Form a contiguous land bridge between two nations via neighboring borders.
6. **Find Map**: Tap country boundaries on an interactive world map.
7. **Coat of Arms**: Identify nations from their heraldic emblems.
